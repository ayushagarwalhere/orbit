# User testing kit

Goal: watch **5 people** use Orbit for about 10 minutes each, then write down what they actually said. Record only real words from real sessions. An empty row is better than an invented quote.

## Who to test with
Aim for a mix. Two who fit any one row is fine.

| # | Profile | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Shopper who cares about sustainability | Main audience for the passport page |
| 2 | Shopper who doesn't care much | Tests whether the hoodie story lands without motivation |
| 3 | Someone who works in retail, fashion, logistics or manufacturing | Will spot unrealistic supply-chain steps |
| 4 | A developer or a security-minded person | Tests whether the verifier is convincing |
| 5 | Anyone over 50, or anyone not into tech | Tests the language |

## Before you start (say this, more or less)
> "I'm testing an app, not you. There are no wrong answers. Please think out loud: say what you're looking at, what you expect, and anything that confuses you. I won't help unless you're stuck."

Ask permission before taking notes or recording. Don't record names unless they agree.

## Tasks (don't explain anything first)
1. **Passport.** Open the overview page and click "See a greenwashed product get caught". *Ask: "In your own words, what's wrong with this hoodie?"* Can they name at least one contradiction?
2. **Score.** *"Why does it get the grade it gets?"* Do they find the "Why …/100" breakdown? Does it make sense?
3. **QR moment.** *"Imagine this is a tag in a shop. Would this change whether you buy it?"*
4. **Return.** In the Console, click "Create my key & join", then return a product for its reward. *"What did you just get, and why?"*
5. **Trust (optional; for the technical testers).** Open Verify, load the hoodie and run it. *"Do you believe this result? What would make you believe it more?"*
6. **Tamper (optional).** On the Ledger page, run the "erase an audit" scenario. *"What happened?"*

## Questions afterwards
- "What is this app for, in one sentence?"
- "What was the most confusing moment?"
- "Would you scan a passport like this in a shop? Why or why not?"
- "On a scale of 1–5, how much would you trust a product's green claim more after seeing this page?"
- "If you could change one thing, what would it be?"

## Afterwards
- Pick the **3 quotes** that best show what worked or what didn't. Include at least one critical quote.
- Fix the most common confusion and say so ("3 of 5 testers missed the breakdown, so we moved it above the claims").
- Put the summary in DEVPOST.md under "What we learned", with first names or roles only, and only if the testers agree.
